- **volleyd: the overlap planner.** `volleyd/overlap-plan` weighs every pair of a wave's tasks by
  the source bytes they share (a shared doc at `plan_doc_weight`; `changes/**` and
  `spec/examples/**` never count), clusters the tasks that share a source file, and per cluster
  collapses strongly coupled tasks into one bundled task (up to `plan_bundle_issues_max` issues
  and `plan_bundle_files_max` source files, tests aside), chains a strong pair that cannot bundle
  (bigger first, one chain per task, never against a declared `after`), and caps a big cluster at
  `plan_cluster_in_flight_max` in flight, with a why on every task and cluster. `explain` puts the
  plan in a few lines and `diffPlan` notes where a wave file differs from it. Pure: nothing runs
  it yet; the 26-issue dogfood replay plans to 15 tasks. (#380)
