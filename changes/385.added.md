- **spec, volleyd: the merge train's formation, token and merge commit.** `formTrain` picks a
  merge train when at least two gated tasks wait on a release branch with no merge in flight: up
  to `train_size_max` (4) of the front task's wave, its overlap-plan cluster first, named
  `train-<n>` on the branch `volley/train/<wave>-t<n>`. A train's members show `merging` in the
  merge queue and never take the ordinary turn. The fence allows exactly `gh pr merge <n> --merge
  --match-head-commit <head>` with a `TrainToken` for that PR and head (never `--squash`, and a
  `MergeToken` never allows `--merge`), and `mergeTrain` re-reads the PR before merging as
  `mergePr` does. SPEC §4.6.23 "Merge train" states the rule. Nothing forms a train yet: the
  driver comes with the integrator. (#385)
