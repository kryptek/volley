- **volley-desk: a large wave no longer crashes the Desk on a phone.** The cables' blurred shadow
  and glow took a buffer the size of the box around every cable in device pixels (about 840 MB in
  WebKit for a 23-task wave on a 3x phone). On touch screens, and on any screen once the blur
  would pass 64 MB per group, the cables are drawn flat, with no filter.
