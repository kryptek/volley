- **volley-cli, volleyd: peer readiness no longer keeps a stale doctor result until the service
  restarts.** `volley peer run` re-runs the doctor on the next heartbeat when the volley
  checkout's `package.json`, `bun.lock` or install marker changes, or when `volley peer doctor`
  ran (it writes `~/.volley/peer-doctor-ran`), and re-reads the versions it reports;
  `volley peer doctor --fix` prints `readiness republished` once the service's record carries the
  new run, else how to restart the service. A Repair (`peer_doctor`) folds its report into the
  box's retained machine record and readiness verdict at once, and the box's next heartbeat,
  still carrying its service's older run, no longer undoes it. (#320)
